2. Printer & alignment

These corrections apply to every medication. One dot is 0.083 mm at 300 dpi. If every label is equally far left, move right; save the correction once. Do not change it when selecting a different medication. If the position varies between labels, check paper size, media guides and sensor calibration.

1.00″ stock uses a 1.00″ page. 0.95″ is the inner content area, leaving about 0.64 mm per side. Offsets are rounded to whole printhead dots. Native ZPL uses the Zebra grid (12 dots/mm at 300 dpi; about 305 dots for 1″). Software offsets cannot correct cumulative feed drift.
